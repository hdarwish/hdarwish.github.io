### Notes
- V1 Arabic colloquial uses "تبي/تقدر" (Gulf casual want/can) — no imperative "go buy" framing. Compliant.
- V1 Hook variant B "سؤال: هل يقدر المقيم في الإمارات يستثمر في NASDAQ؟" uses a question, not a directive. Compliant.
- Landing page Option D (social proof framing) is **conditional** — only deploy with real waitlist data. Do not fabricate numbers.

1. UAE is ~90% expat — English is the dominant professional/social language
2. Bilingual hook signals intentional cultural design (not laziness), builds trust with Arabic-speaking audience

4. Most shareable by UAE expat majority on LinkedIn/Instagram
