The 6 filters

Every bullet you send gets checked against the same six questions. Most weak bullets fail three or more.

FILTER 01

Strong lead verb

Does the bullet open with a decisive action verb — not "Responsible for" or "Helped with"?

FILTER 02

Quantified outcome

Is there a number on the result — a %, a $, time saved, or volume shipped?

FILTER 03

Scope & scale

Is the size visible — team headcount, budget, users served, or requests per day?

FILTER 04

Concrete specifics

Are the actual tools, systems, or methods named — instead of "various technologies"?

FILTER 05

Signal over duties

Does it lead with the impact you created — not a restatement of the job description?

FILTER 06

Role alignment

Does the language mirror the target role, so both ATS keyword scans and human readers catch it?
