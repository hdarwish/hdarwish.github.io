Privacy Policy.

Tiny Tell — Baby Cry Analyzer

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Data Collection

Tiny Tell collects audio recordings when you use the cry analysis feature. We also collect your account email if you create an account in future versions.

How Data is Processed

Audio recordings are processed locally on your device and sent to a private ML server running on Mac hardware. The server performs cry classification using an on-device machine learning model — no third-party AI services are used.

Data Retention

Audio files are discarded immediately after inference completes. Your insight history (cry classifications + timestamps) is stored locally on your device only — we do not maintain server-side records of your analyses.

Third Parties

Tiny Tell does not share data with third parties beyond Apple's infrastructure (App Store, iCloud if you enable device backup). We do not use analytics services, ad networks, or external cloud providers.

Your Rights

You can delete all insight history from the app's Settings screen at any time. Since audio is processed transiently and not stored, there is no audio data to delete from our servers.
