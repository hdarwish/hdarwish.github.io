TinyTell
Understand your baby's cries

A private, fast baby cry analyzer for parents and caregivers. Tap record, capture a few seconds of a cry, and get an honest classification of what your baby might need.

Home Support & FAQ Privacy

One-tap recording

Capture a few seconds of a cry with a single tap. No setup, no fiddling at 3 AM.

Honest classification

A custom model trained on real infant cries flags low-confidence predictions instead of guessing.

Private by design

Audio is analyzed on a private server over an encrypted connection and discarded immediately. No third-party LLMs, no ad SDKs.

Insights over time

Your cry history stays on your device, with patterns by day and week to help you spot trends.

⚠ Not a medical device. TinyTell is a caregiver-convenience tool, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for a pediatrician. If your baby is in distress or you have health concerns, contact a medical professional immediately.
