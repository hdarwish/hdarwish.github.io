Privacy Policy

Voga · Effective 1 July 2026

This policy describes what data Voga collects, how it is processed, and how long it is kept. Voga is operated by Hafs Ibrahim as an independent developer.

What Voga collects

Voga stores the information you add to your wardrobe:

  • Photos of clothing items you upload or capture in the app
  • Tags you assign (garment type, colors, formality, season)
  • A log of outfits you mark as worn (dates and garment combinations)
  • A numeric style profile derived from your outfit choices (no personal name or email required)

Voga does not collect your name, email, phone number, or location unless you voluntarily provide them.

Anonymous accounts

You can use Voga without creating a named account. The app creates an anonymous session automatically on first launch so your wardrobe data is persisted across app launches. There is no username or password to enter. If you later delete your account (via Settings → Delete Account), your wardrobe data is removed (see Data retention and deletion below).

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to power Voga features: generating outfit suggestions, tracking what you’ve worn, and surfacing wardrobe gaps. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for purposes unrelated to the app.

Third-party services

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database and file storage, hosted in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Replicate (replicate.com) — garment image analysis (CLIP embeddings). Garment photos are sent to Replicate for processing; Replicate’s privacy policy governs that transmission.
  • Google Gemini — used for background removal on garment photos. Photos are sent to Google’s API for processing.

No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social-media trackers are embedded in Voga.

Data retention and deletion

Your wardrobe data is retained until you delete your account. To delete your account and all associated data, open Voga → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate for your wardrobe and outfit history; any residual session record is cleared within 30 days.

Children

Voga is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email hafs.darwish+voga@gmail.com. Responses typically within 48 hours.
