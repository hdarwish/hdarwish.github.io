Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No sign-up form is required. Voga creates a private, anonymous session for you automatically the first time you open the app, so your wardrobe is saved across launches. There is no username or password to remember.

How do I get outfit suggestions?

Add at least one top, one bottom, and one pair of shoes via the “+” button. Then open the Home tab — Voga will generate a “Today’s Pick” using those pieces, plus a swipeable carousel of more ideas.

How do I add a garment?

Tap the camera icon or go to Wardrobe and tap “Add Garment.” Take a photo or choose one from your library. Voga processes the image and asks you to confirm a name, garment type, and tags.

Why does the app need camera and photo permissions?

Voga uses the camera and photo library solely so you can photograph the clothing in your own wardrobe. Photos are used only to build your private closet and generate outfit suggestions — never for advertising or tracking.

Why is my Home screen empty?

You need at least one top, one bottom, and one pair of shoes in your wardrobe before Voga can suggest an outfit. Add some garments and pull to refresh.

How do I remove a garment?

In the Wardrobe tab, long-press any garment card and select Archive to remove it from outfit suggestions.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This immediately removes your wardrobe, outfit history, uploaded photos, and all associated data. The action cannot be undone.

How are outfit suggestions generated?

Voga uses visual AI to understand the pieces in your closet and pair them into outfits that make sense together. Suggestions are ideas to choose from — the more you add, the better they get.
