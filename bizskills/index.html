Gmail, Google Calendar, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Notion, Airtable, Stripe, and more. If a tool has an API, we can connect it. Pro tier includes custom integrations.

Yes. The $199 setup fee is one-time. The $29/month is for ongoing support and skill updates. Cancel anytime — skills remain in your Claude account, but you lose support and updates.
